# ANL APS Sector 6 Small Angle X-ray Scattering (SAXS) - October 2025

Argonne National Labs (ANL), Advanced Photon Source (APS), Sector 6, X-ray diffraction (XRD) Data collected in Oct 2025 by J.L. Yarger and C. Benmore.
There was two major sets of samples during this beamtime, biopolymers / silk fibers and amorphous pharmaceutical materials.
