# Argonne National Laboratory (ANL) Advanced Photon Source (APS) Sector 12ID-B - October 2025 

Prof. Jeffery L. Yarger (jyarger@proton.me)

Dr. Xiaobing Zuo (zuox@anl.gov)

The [12-ID-B beamline](https://www.aps.anl.gov/Beamlines/Beamline-Directory/110) is dedicated to SAXS including simultaneous SAXS/WAXS, GISAXS, and time-resolved SAXS for both solids and solutions. The beamline is equipped with furnaces, oven, gas and liquid flow systems, Linkam stages, and home-made multisample heater. Horizontal and vertical mirrors with 2 sets of slits deliver photons higher than 1E12 in the size of beam about 200 (H) x 40(V) um^2. The beam size can be slitted down to the size of about 10x10 um^2 if needed.
